Chainsaw Mail

Follow-ups that send themselves, from your own Gmail.

An Android app for anyone who writes to many people and needs to follow up: a job hunt, a sales pitch, clients who have gone quiet.

Write once. A template fills in each person's name and company, and can carry your resume or a brochure.

Chain it. Email 1 today, a follow-up in 4 days, a last note after a week. Each follow-up goes as a reply in the same thread.

Stop when they answer. When someone replies, mark them Replied and their chain stops.

Your Gmail, your emails

Emails go from your own Gmail account, so they sit in your Sent folder and replies land in your inbox as usual. Chainsaw Mail only sends: it never reads your inbox. It never sells your data and shows no ads. Read the privacy policy for exactly what it keeps.

Sort your people

Put contacts in groups (Companies, Clients - textiles, Recruiters), paste a whole list at once, and put a whole group on a chain in one go. A daily limit keeps your Gmail account in good standing.
