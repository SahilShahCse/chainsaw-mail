Chainsaw Mail

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Chainsaw Mail is an Android app made by Sahil Shah in India. It lets you write emails once, chain them days apart and send them from your own Gmail account, and it stops a person's chain when they reply. This page says what the app collects, why, where it is kept and how to delete it. Questions: sahilshah.apps@gmail.com.

What the app collects and why

  • Your Google account: your name, email address and Google account ID, to sign you in.
  • Gmail access, only when you connect Gmail in the app:
    • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): to send the emails you write or put on a chain, from your own address.
    • Read your email (gmail.readonly): to notice replies and bounces to the emails the app sent. For each new message the app reads only its headers (who sent it, its subject and its thread). It opens the words of a message only when it is an answer from one of your contacts, and keeps that answer (without the quoted email under it) so you can read it in the app and see that the person replied. Other emails are not kept.
    The permission is kept as a token in a private part of our database that only our server functions can read.
  • What you put in the app: your contacts (name, email, company, city, phone, notes, group), templates and the files you attach to them, chains, your signature and settings, and a copy of each email sent through the app.
  • Your phone's notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), to tell you when someone replies.

The app does not read the contacts on your phone, does not track your location, shows no ads and has no advertising or analytics tracking.

Google user data

Chainsaw Mail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Gmail data is used only to send your emails and to show you who replied or bounced, inside the app.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except the service providers below that run the app for us.
  • No person reads it, unless you ask us for help with a specific email and agree to it, or it is needed for security or by law.
  • It is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Where it is kept

Your data is kept by Supabase (database, file storage and server functions) in Mumbai, India, on Amazon Web Services. Notifications go through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS), and each account can reach only its own records.

Who sees it

Only you. We do not sell or share your data. The people you email receive the emails you send them, as with any email.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your data is kept while you have an account. You can delete the account in the app at any time: Settings, the account button at the top, Delete my account. That removes your contacts, templates, files, chains, stored emails and settings, and revokes the app's access to your Gmail, right away. Our host's backups roll off within 30 days. You can also ask us to delete it, or remove the Gmail access yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to sahilshah.apps@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We answer within 30 days. These rights include those under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

Chainsaw Mail is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date change with it.
