Delete your Chainsaw Mail account

In the app (right away)

  1. Open Chainsaw Mail and tap Settings (the gear on Home).
  2. Tap the account button at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Delete everything.

Without the app

Write to sahilshah.apps@gmail.com from the Google address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Chainsaw Mail account". The account is deleted within 7 days and you get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything: your account, contacts and groups, templates and their files, chains, the copies of emails sent through the app and the replies kept with them, your settings and notification token. The app's access to your Gmail is revoked. Nothing is kept, except that our host's backups roll off within 30 days. The emails themselves stay in your own Gmail.

Only the Gmail access

To keep the account but stop the app reaching your Gmail, use Disconnect Gmail in Settings, or remove Chainsaw Mail at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
